AI evaluation

Horizon dataset audits

Find the broken tasks before a lab does.

AI labs buy task datasets and training environments to test and train their models. Some of those tasks cannot be solved. Some graders give full marks to wrong answers. Some environments fail to start. Each of these makes the score wrong.

My contribution: Building the audit tool behind Horizon’s public quality index. It runs every task in a clean container, submits an empty answer and the supplied solution, and challenges the grader with incomplete work. Every finding links to its proof. Our audit of public datasets from major vendors confirmed 29 broken tasks.

I also design long-horizon coding benchmarks. Their tasks are generated by code, and a script grades every answer exactly.
